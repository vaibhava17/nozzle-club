From a name to your keys in four steps

  1. 1

    You design it

    Type your text, pick a shape, two colours and a finish. The preview is the exact model we print.

  2. 2

    We slice it

    Your model is cut into 0.2 mm layers, about twenty layers for every 4 mm of keychain.

  3. 3

    It prints

    A 210 °C nozzle lays down plant-based PLA one line at a time. Most keychains take under an hour.

  4. 4

    It ships

    We clip on the ring, check every edge by hand and send it out within two days.
